The designer's intentions (2026-11-26, from their notes with ChatGPT): Enfina is a **mobile vanguard** - momentum and opportunity,
Trailblaze as the foundation; "Cyrus gets around obstacles by finding ways around them. Enfina gets around obstacles by moving
straight through them." Narrative: she thinks she must become an imposing warrior like Theron; her real strength is reaching
people, protecting them, creating openings. Secondary stat Intellect, but she is not social or manipulative.

Built 2026-11-26 (docs/proposals_2026-11-26_enfina.md "Chosen, and built"; CLAUDE.md "Through, not around"): things with health (the
TileSet's Health; anybody depletes it, she breaks anything at once), Burn Through, Splinters, Breakthrough (fire path three wide),
Shoulder Through (once a turn), Intercept, Grab as a secondary action that carries allies, Weak Points, stronger Blazed Path burn,
smashing on maps; Momentum ("Planted") taken out. All "core", not path-only.

Paths, 2026-11-27 ("The paths sound good, but i don't want juggernaut to be about her being immovable. ill need to think about it
/ do the rest"): Vanguard got Firebrand (41) in place of Daunting Roar; Warden of Cover got Daunting Roar and a reworked
Interceptor (Step In 3 tiles, burning or not, no walk). **Juggernaut unchanged and open: never pitch it as the immovable warrior.**
Planted has no home (files in docs/set_aside/passives/). Vanguard name kept "for now" (designer, 2026-11-27). Enemy Grab is
secondary too (designer's yes, 2026-11-27): Barbarian via ai_secondary_attack, Glutton grabs then bites.

Refused: Wood Burns, Carry Out, Opening, the rest of Dragon's Wake, Afterburn, Flashpoint, Reads the Line.

**Why:** she felt mechanically thin next to Cyrus, Prometheus and Alithia; players loved Trailblaze.

**How to apply:** new Enfina ideas should move her through things, reach allies, or make openings - not plain damage or
tanking. Wait for the designer on the Juggernaut. Numbers and levels are PROPOSAL ([[balance-numbers-are-the-users]]). Related:
