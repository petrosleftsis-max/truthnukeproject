The designer, 2026-11-13: "the real size of the human size creatures will be the size of the idle animation of cyrus".
Cyrus's idle frames are 384 x 384 (`art/characters/cyrus/idle/`), the figure in them about 330 pixels tall, 1.3 tiles of 256.
Alithia's present frames (192 x 192) and other smaller figures are not the final size.

**Why:** the art is being redrawn to one scale; anything sized against a person has to be judged against Cyrus, not the others.

**How to apply:** size heights, slots and effects against a ~330 px person (wall lift, cutaway radius, roofs, health bars,
art-slot sizes written for the artist). Take screenshots with Cyrus in view. Related: [[two-and-a-half-d-chosen]],
[[placeholder-assets-with-slots]].
