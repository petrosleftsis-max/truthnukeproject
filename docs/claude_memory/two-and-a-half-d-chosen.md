On 2026-11-13 the designer and their artist chose 2.5D as A, B and C of docs/proposals_2026-11-07.md section 22, and it was built
on 2026-11-13 to 15 (Job 1 of docs/fable_brief.md). `docs/2_5d_plan.md` has the whole story and what is still asked of the
designer; `docs/depth_on_maps.md` is the guide for maps and the artist's sizes; CLAUDE.md "Depth" has the code.

What the designer said about it, worth remembering when touching it:
- They like the cutaway (seeing people through what covers them), the tree ("such a nice feeling of depth"), roofs that fade.
- Barrels are objects, not raised ground: drawn bigger, floor flat.
- A thing drawn off its tile bothers them ("the door isnt on the same tile as its drawing"): what can be used must sit on its tile.
- A backdrop must have no visible end and go all round the map.
- Walls, an arch and a tree should "pop out" of mist: only thick fog goes over them (2026-11-14).
- A door must read by which way its wall runs: a door face turned to the viewer in a side wall looked wrong, and an open leaf
  across the way looked like it blocked it. A door is as tall as its wall.
- 2026-11-15: a shut door facing the viewer should be seen standing, its front art over the lifted part too (not a big top
  over a short front); a door in a side wall should hug its building's side, meeting the roof, and open its handle sits at the
  outside end. The thickest fog goes over everything; the town below stays in every gap.
- They want to walk close to walls: a small hitbox at the feet.
- They wanted Cyrus's glowing eyes gone, NOT the lighter dark when he leads (they first wrote "night eyes visual effect" and I
  removed the wrong one; it was put back).
- They call the stage's pieces by what they look like (the arch slab was "the door") and can misname an ability ("might eyes"):
  when a remark could mean two things on screen, say which reading was taken, and prefer the one they can see. Interpretation mine.

**Why:** it keeps all existing art and rules, and adds depth where it shows most.

**How to apply:** view only, never a rule. Walls are 192 tall and raised ground 96, their picks of 2026-11-16 from pictures
beside Cyrus ([[human-size-is-cyrus-idle]]); for a choice of looks they want to see the options as pictures ("show me
examples?"), one recommended. Open with them as of 2026-11-16: the feet hitbox (they are testing it); the third round's doors were approved and committed 2026-11-15. Fable ran out of usage on 2026-11-15 (resets 2026-11-19); this work continued in a Claude Code
session on Opus. Related: [[interface-look-direction]], [[placeholder-assets-with-slots]].
