# Messengers of Truth: the four player characters

Who Cyrus, Prometheus, Enfina and Alithia are, what they are like, and what they can do, in the story and in play. The game is a grid tactical RPG in development, and this describes it as of 7 November 2026.

**If you are an AI reading this:** this is a briefing from the game's designer, for thinking and writing about these characters. Keep to what it says. Much of the story is undecided, and each character has a list of what has not been said yet. Treat those as open: ask, or offer ideas labelled as ideas, and do not state them as facts.

**How to read it:**

- **Quotes** are the game's own text, word for word, or the designer's own words where it says so.
- ***Reading:*** marks an interpretation made while writing this document. It is not something the designer has stated, so feel free to disagree with it.
- **New** marks moves the designer approved on 7 November 2026, built that day, and not yet tried by players.
- **Every number is a trial value.** Balance is still being tested, so nothing in the story should lean on an exact figure.
- **Looks** are described from the current in-game art, which may change.
- **The story exists in pieces:** an opening, a middle stretch, and a scene from near the end, plus some test stages. The stealth stages are test material, though their lines show how the characters sound.

**Content note:** Alithia's late scene deals with a suicide attempt and its aftermath.

**Companion file:** `chatgpt_narrative_context.md` has the wider world and every conversation in the game word for word. It dates from October, so where it differs on abilities, this document is the newer one.

## 1. The world in one minute

- **The Dome:** the world lives under it, and nobody remembers when it came to be. About thirty years ago the sky inside it "became pixelated".
- **The Divine** ("It"): speaks through prophets, and people trust It more than their governments. In the Prophecy Miracle, prophets across the whole Dome said the same words at the same moment: "The world is in danger. The Dome is there to protect you. But you will not need its protection forever. The Great Propagation will happen."
- **The Hero:** "a young pure man of virtue, ready to lead his party under the direct guidance of the Divine". The story opens on the day of his Accolade. He is none of these four, and he has not appeared yet.
- **Idols and Idology:** magic is the use of Idols. Not everyone has one, and it is very hard to learn. Stories say Yaldabaoth gave humanity this "Gift of Idolatry". Idol damage is the game's holy damage.
- **Gates:** casters draw power through them, weakest to strongest: World, Hermes, Pandora, Charon, Tartarus, Nemesis, Sophia, Ogdoad, Gnosis, Yaldabaoth.
- **The Holy Order:** clergy, priests, monks and knights. Enfina could not be trusted to become one of its knights. Alithia is one of its most talented Idol users, and her questioning has split its clergy.
- **The Wolf:** "It who hungers endlessly." It was known to exist and known to be contained, and it turns up loose in the sewers in the opening.
- **Truth and forgetting:** Alithia's name is the Greek ἀλήθεια, truth. The level cap, 64, is the letter-sum of that word. Levels past 55, marked ΛΗΘΗ (Lethe, forgetting), stay hidden until the last chapter. The game has five chapters, ending at levels 18, 33, 45, 55 and 64.

## 2. How play works, enough to read the abilities

- **A turn:** on the grid, somebody can walk, take a **main action** and take a **secondary action**. A **reaction** can fire outside their turn.
- **Attributes:** Physical, Mindfulness, Intellect, Self and Defense. Each character leans on two of them.
- **Damage figures:** x1.0 is a plain blow, x1.5 half as hard again, and so on. Moves scale with an attribute.
- **Contests:** some moves are one attribute against another, and a loss means nothing happens.
- **Leverage:** Persuade, Deceive and Intimidate work only on somebody the party has something on: their faction known, one of their kind questioned, or a clue about them.
- **Spells and gates:** a spell comes when its gate opens, at levels 1, 8, 14, 21, 28, 34, 41, 47, 54 and 61, and each casting spends one use of a gate. Only Prometheus and Alithia have gates. Casters choose which spells to prepare after a long rest (5 at level 1, up to 35 at level 64).
- **Tethers:** some spells last only while the caster holds a tether, a bar that frays each turn and when they are hurt. They can let go at any time.
- **Rests:** a short rest brings back some health and limited moves. A long rest brings back everything.
- **Paths:** at level 24 each character chooses one of three paths, and can change it at a long rest.
- **Light:** every tile is Bright, Dim or Dark. In the Dark you can be seen only by those who see in it, or from very close. Darkness is also cover: nobody sees into it or past it, except those who see in the dark.
- **Outside fights:** the party walks maps in real time, and guards watch by line of sight. Being caught starts a fight. Many moves also work on a map, and only Cyrus can sneak.
- **Fights rarely need to end in killing:** the fallen are knocked out, enemies can flee, surrender or be bribed, and prisoners can be questioned, let go or handed over.
- **Two players can share the party**, each steering some of the four, on one screen or over a network.

## 4. Cyrus Rowe (he/him)

**Looks:** cat ears and a tail, brown hair with a small tied lock, amber eyes, a dark coat, and always the red scarf. A poster in a guard room reads: "WANTED: a cat-eared thief in a red scarf. A good likeness, sadly." He is so far the only one with drawn dialogue portraits, and their expressions are everyday, excited, grin, nervous sweat, scared, serious, smile with sweat, and smug.

**In the game's words:**

> A swift cunning cat with a bunch of tricks up his sleeves to hide from enemies and hunt down his targets.
>
> An unfortunate circumstance which resulted in his mother's passing and him running away from home, has caused him to be stranded and struggling to survive since his teenage years. These harsh circumstances have forced him to develop the skills and greed of a thief.
>
> And yet, despite the cruelty of reality, he still keeps on to his red scarf. The scarf that reminds him of the oath he swore to himself of becoming a hero.
>
> These childish ambitions have lead him into a secret group, which has the intention to steal and weaken those who grow too strong.

### Personality

- **Teases first.** Caught by Enfina with a stolen relic: "Now, that'd just kill the fun, don't you think?"
- **Really greedy.** Finding supplies the guards left behind: "Now, how could I ever leave something so useful just laying around like that? It would be such a huge waste~." Looking at laboratory machinery: "is there some part of it we can take and sell for a lot?"
- **Really generous, and won't make a thing of it.** At the empty beds of the sewer's homeless: "Well they should count their lucky stars that I happen to pass by. Here. That should keep you fed for a few more days. ... Wherever you may be."
- **Talks a lot, and narrates.** He tells the player about the Dome and the prophecy himself. Enfina: "You don't need to narrate it!"
- **Shows his fear.** He stammers when surprised or frightened: "D-didn't expect to meet you here!" and "I-I recognise those faces... That's... Ah..."
- **Does not leave.** In the sewers he could run, and he stays to help. Enfina: "This won't help your case, you know." Cyrus: "Never counted on it."
- **Proud of the craft.** After a job: "The far end of the lab, and not a single alarm. That's how it's done." And: "Robes off, and out the back. Just another quiet night."
- **Sceptical about the powers above him.** "I guess even the Divine is too busy handling other matters to assist these people."
- **Quick to back down when he has hurt somebody.** When Alithia shuts down his question about the barrel, he says only "Ah, sorry."
- **In a late memory, the oath at its loudest and its breaking point:** "A hero never backs down from saving one more person! No matter what! I will never leave you behind!" and then "If my demise can end all suffering. Then I... Why can I not do it...?!"

*Reading:* the jokes and the greed are how he survives, and the scarf is what he has not given up. He seems to do the heroic thing while pretending it is an accident, right up until the story takes away the option of pretending.

### How he talks

Loose, running sentences, rhetorical questions and asides. "Hah" and "Bah", and a smug "~" on a line now and then. He wants things plain ("Human language please!"), and he often ends a job on a dry one-liner. He is more open in narration and thoughts, which are often in brackets, than in what he says to people.

### What has not been said

- What the relic is, and who the secret group are. He mentions "the others", away on a different mission.
- How his mother died ("an unfortunate circumstance").
- Whose faces he recognised on the mangled creatures in the sewers.

### In play

**The designer:** "Cyrus has a lot of mobility and utility capabilities, getting a lot of ways to control enemies while being able to be flexible by very easily using any item."

**The game:** "By having high initiative and movement, he gets to decide how a combat begins and where he is needed to go... This makes him the only member who can get away from the team to take care of potent but frail targets, such as casters!"

He has the highest initiative and no magic of his own, yet he can throw any spell in a bottle. A good Cyrus turn is often two things where others get one: run and strike, drink and shoot, strike and slip away. His poisons and burns add up over a fight, and his dark moves make him strongest where the party's lights are not. On maps he is the heart of stealth.

### Abilities

**Blows and shots**

- **Rapier** (level 1; melee, x1.5; also a reaction when an enemy leaves his reach).
- **Gun** (1; range 20, x1.0): a shot at a lamp, a torch or any other light puts it out, quietly. **New:** on a map, a shot at a guard carrying a torch puts it out from afar, silently, and he wanders off to relight it, walking in the dark meanwhile (on the Gun for now; the designer will move it to a later ranged move).
- **Blink Strike** (1; x0.8): arrives on a tile within 4 and cuts everything beside it.
- **Poison Dart** (8; x0.9): poisons.
- **Burning Shot** (16; x0.8): burns, and burns stronger from 32.
- **Venom Fan** (24; a cone, x0.7): poisons everybody in front of him.
- **Warning Shot** (32; x0.9): slows their advance.
- **Catalyst** (64; once a short rest): every lingering condition of his, on every enemy, goes off at once for twice what it had left, and then starts again.

**A thief's tools**

- **Steal** (1): lifts everything an enemy has to hand and keeps it, ready to use.
- **Deceive** (1; needs leverage, Self against Intellect): convinces them one of their own has sold them out, and their next turn goes on the nearest ally of theirs.
- **Stealth** (1): slips out of sight while no enemy can see him, and stays hidden until one can.
- **Slip Past** (1): nothing can react to him for the rest of the turn.
- **Run** (1): doubles his movement.
- **Shadow Step** (8; secondary): from one Dim or Dark tile to another within 4, with a clear line. Nobody can react, and nobody sees him go.
- **Put On Disguise**: while hidden, and with a disguise that would pass among them, he walks in plain sight until he acts against them.

**What he always is (passives)**

- **Light Footed:** Stealth, Slip Past and Run can be his secondary action, so he can Run twice in a turn or slip away after striking. **Elusive** says the same for Slip Past.
- **Quick Hands:** items can be his secondary action, so he can drink a potion and still attack.
- **Nade Juggler:** throws any Gate Nade (a spell in a bottle), whatever is in it, as a secondary action. Everybody else can only throw ones whose spell they know or could learn.
- **Low Shot:** a prone enemy gets no cover from his ranged moves.
- **Night Eyes:** he sees in the dark.
- **Silhouette** (8): somebody in Bright, shot from the Dark, takes a fifth more.
- **Cat Burglar** (1, 24): extra movement, more when he starts in cover. From 24 he climbs onto high ground from anywhere, and up walls or pillars to the floor above.
- **Agile** (8, 32): takes less damage in cover, and stepping into cover sets off nothing.
- **DoT Master** (16, 41): his poisons and burns hit harder and last longer.
- **Synergy** (54, 64): the whole party hits anybody carrying a harmful condition harder, and harder still if it is one of his.

**On a map**

He is the only one who can creep unseen; everybody else walking close to a guard is noticed. He hides in hiding spots, throws things to draw guards off, takes guards down and drags bodies, picks pockets, wears disguises (a priest's robes) and talks his way through, drugs a supper, plants a forged letter or a stolen ring, and shoots out lights. When Prometheus casts Company Unseen, everybody else turns invisible and walks in his footsteps.

### Paths (level 24)

- **Skirmisher.** "The rapier. Cyrus strikes first, from the flank, and is gone before it can be answered - and is never quite where the blow lands." Steps away free after a Rapier strike, hits harder beside allies, deals double on his first hit on each enemy, leaves afterimages in cover, and at 64 can use the Rapier as his secondary action too.
- **Saboteur.** "The ammunition. Cyrus loads rounds that chill and corrode, fires two at once, sets off what is already burning in them - and lets it spread." Frost Round, Corrosive Round, Combined Load, and at 64 his conditions spread to whoever stands beside their victim.
- **Trapper.** "Cyrus never fights a fight that is not already rigged: a bomb in hand, the ground set under them, and whoever walks into it held there." Free bomb, Snare, Tripwire, and at 64 Minefield.

### Where the story and the play meet

- He steals and he gives, in play as in the story: Steal takes everything an enemy has, and his Quick Hands turn it straight to use for the party.
- The thief who "weakens those who grow too strong" fights by wearing down, with poison, burns and the Catalyst, more than by one great blow.
- "Where I hide, you hide" (to Enfina, in a stealth stage) is also Company Unseen: everybody walks in his footsteps.
- *Reading:* the only member who can safely leave the team is also the one who swore "I will never leave you behind". Play keeps tempting him to do what he has promised not to.

## 5. Prometheus Iliopoulos (he/him)

**Looks:** messy brown hair, an open white coat over a waistcoat and tie, usually an open book in his hand.

**In the game's words:**

> A messy pragmatic scholar who can never be seen without a book and a drink.
>
> He was an up and rising star in Idology from his late teen years. This lead him to quickly get into high and important research. However... It is now a painful memory that only destroys him further and further as time passes.
>
> Despite it all, or perhaps because of it all, he is now a really warm and caring person that, despite not saying it out loud, would always be there for you.

**His name** is the Titan's, who brought fire to people and was punished for it.

### Personality

- **Can't help explaining.** Found a book on Idology, he lectures until Cyrus cuts him off with "Human language please!"
- **Dry and self-mocking, and quick to come down to your level.** His answer to Cyrus: "... Is good book."
- **Enjoys setting up a joke.** Cyrus asks if they can strip the machinery to sell. "Are you crazy? Now you're just being ridiculous, Cyrus." Alithia: "Thank you!" Then: "Almost half of those small parts over there would obviously sell for a fortune."
- **Ducks away from pain, and uses a drink to do it.** When something in a barrel stops him cold: "I... Yeah. It's nothing." And then: "Haha, thank you. Don't worry, I just remembered I need to restock on some drinks on the way back."
- **Careful and practical when it counts.** "Let's make sure we didn't miss anything behind us first. If we alert the place, we cannot be sure we can head back."
- **Shows care sideways.** To Alithia at her worst: "Well, I can't exactly come up with a wise idiom that would pick you up from the slumps, but I definetely can share a drink with you. Haha, this is only half an excuse to drink."
- **Believes the evidence, even when it hurts.** "No, there is no doubting it... This truly is the Divine. Their conclusions and reasonings... are so flawless." And, in a memory at the end: "I cannot refute any of these..."
- **How Alithia sees him:** "You damn alcoholic. You can't even move on from the past. And yet... you found beauty in remembering. You accepted the pain and allowed yourself to hold onto it."

*Reading:* he is the party's warmest member and its most damaged, and the warmth seems to be the form the damage took. He is honest about facts and evasive about himself. His humour works as a door he shuts gently.

### How he talks

Scholarly and complete when he is on his subject, then short and wry. "Haha" often comes just after something painful. He is quick to approve of others ("Efficient as ever", to Cyrus). When the party decides things he says little: "The watch can have them."

### What has not been said

- What his research was, and what happened.
- What is in the barrel at the laboratory.
- What the line on his workbench means, the only one left among crossed-out notes, underlined three times: "The seal remembers how you hum, not who you are."
- What he has concluded about the Divine, and what he "cannot refute".

### In play

**The designer:** "Prometheus is the ultimate aoe damager and controller, with a different kind of utility to the rest, while having every other type of magic." And: "Prometheus is supposed to go down fairly easily if not careful."

**The game:** "The hardest unit to use well, he has the most potent capabilities in the party. Yet they are limited by resource and his frail frame makes him easy to be killed in but an instant. It is more important for him to stay alive, rather than cast spells earlier! ... To make the most of him is to make the most of all!"

He has by far the least health. His power lies in choosing which gate to spend on what, and where. **He learns spells only from books,** found as loot or bought, or by using up a Gate Nade, and only those that are his. He also has a few small shots that cost no gate. His fires and winds linger on the ground, so he shapes where people can stand. Every spell he casts makes the next burn hotter.

### Abilities

**Costing no gate**

- **Fire Burst, Zephyr Dart, Caucasus Shard** (x0.7): small shots of fire, wind and rock.
- **Study** (secondary): takes the measure of an enemy, health, attributes, weaknesses and moves, for the party to read.
- **Hack** (main): reaches into a spell a foe is winding up, through a minigame. Win, and it comes to nothing and a boss reels. Lose, and it may go off early.
- **Slip Past, Run.**

**Gate of World** (level 1)

- **Fire Blast, Wind Shot, Rock Throw** (x1.2): his plain blasts.
- **Quicken:** grants an ally speed.
- **Turn Aside** (reaction): he takes half of a blow of fire, water, wind, earth, poison or plain force.
- **Flare:** a big area is Bright for two of his turns, and everybody hidden there is seen. On a map every guard hears it.
- **Dancing Lights:** three small lights he can send about with a secondary action, for ten of his turns.

**Gate of Hermes** (8)

- **Burner** (area, x1.7 fire): the ground burns for three of his turns, hurting whoever walks it.
- **Crystalise** (area, x1.0 earth): hindering stones, so each step costs twice.
- **Wind Swoon** (area, x1.0 wind): a raging wind that throws out whoever starts or ends a turn in it.
- **Gust Blast** (x1.2) and **Wind Vacuum** (x0.2): push somebody away, or suck them closer.
- **Light Void** (tether): the light goes out of the ground, and anybody in it is invisible to those who cannot see in the dark.
- **For allies:** **Hermes Step** (put them anywhere within 6), **Hover** (they fly), **Cling** (they climb as Cyrus does), **Growth** (a size bigger for three turns), **Invisibility** (self or ally; on a map, through the Gates of Iris).
- **Mist Step** (secondary): he is gone, and there again on any free tile within 6.

**Gate of Pandora** (14)

- **Wildfire** (area, x2.1 fire): runs through all of them and keeps burning.
- **Ravenous Dark** (area, x1.2 psychic): a dark that bites each turn. Steps cost twice, and those in it are invisible to whoever cannot see in the dark.
- **Mesmerise** (area, tether): enemies who lose the contest stand and stare.
- **Kindled Hound** (tether): a hound of embers that fights for him.
- **Dispel Magic:** undoes what spells did to somebody, or a spell's zone.
- **InstaHack** (reaction): tears a spell apart the instant a foe casts it. They lose the spell and the action, but not the gate.
- **Great Snare** (only out of a fight): a great magical trap that opens the fight with a blast when a guard sets it off.

**The deep gates**

- **Tartarus** (28): **Tartarus Flame** (x3.1 fire, one enemy), and **Company Unseen** (only out of a fight: everybody but Cyrus invisible, walking in his footsteps).
- **Sophia** (41): **Ire of Sophia** (x4.2 lightning): "Wisdom's anger".
- **Ogdoad** (47): **Illusory Dragon:** "Shadow drawn out of the Ogdoad and given a dragon's shape". Blows miss it, nearby foes are gripped by fear, and it breathes what it is told to.
- **Gnosis** (54): **Gnostic Tempest** (x5.4 plasma): "A tempest of knowing".
- **Yaldabaoth** (61): **Stolen Sun** (x6.0 fire): "The fire he stole from the gods, let go at once: a sun on the battlefield."

**What he always is (passives)**

- **Prying Mind:** he can hack wards, machines, locks and spells being wound up. Higher Intellect gives more tries.
- **Kindling** (1, 16, 54): each spell he casts in a fight makes the next 5% hotter, up to 15%, 30%, then 45%.
- **Elemental Affinity** (8, 32): more damage on anybody weak to the element, and on anybody already taking lingering damage.
- **Contest Insight** (24, 41): he reads a contest before it starts, and does better still against the afflicted.
- **Elemental Mastery** (64): one more casting through his deepest gate.
- **Meditate:** at a short rest, once until a long rest, he brings back castings he has spent.

**Hacking outside fights:** wards, seals and machines are shown as rules on a sheet, and he edits them in minigames. A badly balanced edit makes "the Demiurge" notice. His own forgotten strongbox is one such puzzle.

**New** (7 November 2026)

- **Shrink** (Hermes in a fight, Iris on a map; tether): a size smaller. A foe loses reach and grip, and an ally on a map fits through small gaps.
- **Transposition** (Pandora): two people in reach swap places, contested if unwilling.
- **Grease** (World): ground that knocks down whoever walks it, and burns if fire touches it.
- **Wall of Stone** (Pandora, tether): placed by two points, a real wall that stops both sides and can be broken.
- **False Life** (World): health on top of his own for a while.
- **Withering Touch** (World): a weak blow, and they cannot be mended until their next turn.
- **Blight** (Pandora, tether): an area where everything standing rots.
- **Saved for later:** spells deeper than Pandora, such as Flesh to Stone, Polymorph, Singularity, Conjure Elemental and Marionette.
- **Open:** the designer is unsure where necromancy should go. One idea on the table is rare, forbidden books for him, tied to the research he won't speak of, with Alithia having something to say about it.

### Paths (level 24)

- **Elemental Vanguard.** "The blade. Prometheus closes in, quick on his feet, and fights with swords of fire, ice, storm and stone - each blow a spell." Flame, Frost, Storm and Stone Edge, Flame Fan, and more movement.
- **Elemental Tactician.** "The ward. Prometheus shapes the fight rather than ending it: shielding his friends, holding his enemies where he wants them - and, once, opening every gate at once." Ward (a reaction that takes 40% off a hit on an ally), overheals, and at 64 Grand Design.
- **Elemental Conductor.** "The overcharge. Prometheus pushes every spell past where it should stop: through deeper gates, from one enemy to the next, and once, twice over." Storm Cloud, Overcharge, Chain, Resonance, and at 64 Conduit.

### Where the story and the play meet

- He rewrites the rules, as a scholar would: wards are sheets of rules, and he edits them.
- The Titan's fire: his deepest spell is called Stolen Sun, and fire is his first element.
- He learns from books, one by one, while Alithia grows into hers by levelling.
- *Reading:* Kindling (each spell burning hotter) on the frailest body in the party looks a lot like the character: giving more and more, with very little holding him up.

## 6. Enfina Moonwish (she/her)

**Looks:** long pale lilac hair tied back with a red ribbon, a cropped top, dark trousers, tall boots and a long red sash. She carries an enormous greatsword on her shoulder.

**In the game's words:**

> A city guard who has trained and studied to perfectly respond to any combat situation.
>
> Not born in favourable circumstances, she was faced with a lot of disadvantages. Unable to be trusted to become a knight of the Holy Order, too useful to just leave her out of the picture, yet still faced with uncertainty. An uncertainty that she also sees in herself and how cut out she truly is for the path she chose.
>
> And yet, she still raises her sword. She still trains unrelentlessly, to perform as well as possible, even if no one has any intention of recognising and rewarding her for it with a higher rank.
>
> But Enfina's intentions are pure. For she does not raise her sword for herself. She raises it for everyone else.

### Personality

- **Few words.** "You don't need to narrate it!" "Nobody left to ask. Let's keep moving."
- **Threatens plainly.** "Try anything funny and you'll lose more than just an arm."
- **Duty before the chase.** She drops her pursuit of Cyrus at once when people are in danger: "Just get out of my way, Cyrus. I don't have time for this." He works out why: "You're protecting the sewer residents, aren't you?"
- **Steps forward without being asked.** "Allow me to take the lead. If needed, I'll carve a path ahead."
- **Pragmatic about the law she serves, and dry about it.** On stripping the laboratory: "Yeah. We could come back later." Alithia: "Aren't you a city guard?!" Enfina: "Eh, no law really protects the unlawful."
- **Fair to people she has to fight.** "We have no quarrel with sellswords."
- **Quietly gentle.** With Alithia at her lowest: "I'm sorry for only listening. I do not know what to say. But I can stay here and listen. If that helps you."
- **Carries what she has done, and has come to a hard peace with it.** "There is no use trying to count the value of anyone's soul. And yet these hands have taken lives. In the end there are some situations that do not have an answer and still demand for one. I believe you can never be wrong no matter which answer you choose."
- **What she wants, in the end:** "I just want you all to be okay..."

*Reading:* she is uncertain of herself but never of what to do next. She has stopped waiting for anybody to tell her she is good enough, and she goes on working anyway. Her bluntness is not coldness: when it counts she is the one who stays and listens.

### How she talks

Short sentences, often one line. Little or no decoration, and no explaining herself. Her humour is so dry it can pass as a plain statement ("Eh, no law really protects the unlawful."). When she does say more, it is careful, considered and a little formal.

### What has not been said

- What "unfavourable circumstances" she was born into, and why she could not be trusted to become a knight.
- How she and Cyrus first met. She knows his name when she catches him.
- Which lives she means by "these hands have taken lives".

### In play

**The game:** "Capable of dealing the most damage and take the most hit, she is an invaluable piece that allows her to fend off the mightiest of foe. Her foes fear her reactive skill, so staying close to one will either force them to stay in place, or move and be damaged. As a frontliner, she controls where the fight takes place."

**The designer:** said on 7 November 2026 that the other three now each have a distinct character in play, "But Enfina not so much. Players have been really liking her trailblaze move." So she is the one being deepened right now, around that move.

**The idea behind the new moves** (a proposal the designer approved): *she pays, so that others don't have to, and she knows how you fight.* **Trailblaze** is the one move of hers that asks a real question: she sets herself on fire, losing health each turn, to run faster.

### Abilities

**The sword**

- **Greatsword Attack** (1; melee, x1.0; also a reaction when an enemy leaves her reach). It grows: **Heavy Swing** (x1.5) at 8, and **Titanfall** at 64 (x3.0, and it hits everybody around the target).
- **Light Swing** (1; x1.0): every hit mends a share of her own health, twice that the first time in a fight she strikes a foe at half health or less.
- **Shoving Strike** (1; x1.0): pushes them back.
- **Blink Strike** (1; x0.8): arrives on a tile within 4 and cuts everything beside it.
- **Fire Burst** (1; x0.7): a weak burst of flame, and her only ranged move.
- **Battle Cry** (16; a cone, x0.6): drives them back a tile, off whoever stands behind her.

- **Stunning Blow** (41; x0.8; limited by short rests): they lose their next turn.
- **Sweep Strike** (54; a wide line, x1.4): swings at everybody in range.

**Her body and her training**

- **Trailblaze** (4; secondary): "Blaze your way through." She sets herself alight for five turns: it hurts every turn, and she moves two tiles further.
- **Guard** (1): stays on her guard, defence up until her next turn.
- **Grab** (1; Physical against Physical; also a reaction): holds somebody her size, smaller, or one size bigger. As a reaction, she drags them back as they try to leave.
- **Hurl** (1; x1.0): throws somebody. Throw them into somebody else and both are hurt.
- **Climb** (1): climbs onto something Monstrous or bigger and fights it from on top. It will try to throw her off.
- **Study** (1; secondary): takes the measure of an enemy.
- **Intimidate** (1; needs leverage, Physical against Self): breaks their nerve, leaving them afraid and nearer to running or giving up.
- **Slip Past, Run.**

**What she always is (passives)**

- **Measured Eye:** every blow she lands studies whoever it hits, opening their sheet to the party and sharpening her next blows.
- **Iron Hide** (1, 16, 41): takes less damage from everything. From 41, half of any hit on an ally beside her is hers instead.
- **Bulwark** (8, 54): allies beside her take less, and from 54 her guard reaches two tiles.
- **Momentum** (32, 64): hits harder on a turn she has not walked.

**On a map:** she can break breakable walls, which only she and spells or items can do.

**New** (7 November 2026)

*Fire she pays for:*

- **Blazed Path** (8): while she burns, the tiles she walks stay lit until her next turn. Allies on them walk further, and foes stepping there catch fire.
- **Flashover** (16; secondary): ends her Burn at once in a blast round her, stronger the more Burn she had left.
- **Cauterise** (24; secondary): ends her Burn and mends her for each turn it had left, also taking off Poison or Corroded.
- **Share the Fire** (32): Trailblaze on an ally. They get the speed, and the Burn's damage lands on her.
- **Through the Fire** (41): while burning she walks through fire unharmed, thaws at once, and Fear cannot take hold.
- **Burning Titanfall** (64): Titanfall while she burns leaves the ground it shoved them off burning.

*Trained to respond:*

- **Drills** (8, more at 24 and 41; a passive): new reactions always ready, **Riposte** (strike whoever hits her) and **Step In** (take a blow for an ally beside her), beside the one she has now.
- **Reads the Form** (32): against somebody studied, her first reaction each round does not spend her reaction.

*A city guard's tricks:*

- **Throw a Friend:** Hurl, aimed at a willing ally, lands them up to 3 tiles away, ready to act.
- **Anything Is a Weapon:** Hurl picks up a barrel or a keg as well, and a blast object goes off where it lands.
- **Arrest:** a contested order on somebody whose nerve is nearly gone. They surrender and can be questioned afterwards.
- **Carries Them** (on a map): she drags a body or a fallen ally at full pace.

### Paths (level 24)

- **Vanguard.** "The charge. Enfina burns, runs at them and does not stop: harder after a charge, through everything that would hold her, and on from one fallen to the next." Adrenaline (while burning, her hits deal 30% more and spread the fire), Leading Charge, Daunting Roar, Unstoppable, and at 64 Rampage.
- **Juggernaut.** "The immovable. Enfina drags them in, cuts their legs from under them and pins them where they stand - and nothing moves her." Chain Hook, Hamstring, Pin Down, Immovable, and at 64 Tremor.
- **Warden of Cover.** "The protector. Enfina stands between her friends and harm - frightening it back, guarding them as she guards herself, and taking what gets through." Shield Bash, Covering Stance, Hold the Line, Interceptor, and at 64 Last Wall: while she stands, no blow fells an ally within two tiles. It stops at 1 health, and the rest lands on her.

### Where the story and the play meet

- "She raises it for everyone else" is written all over her kit: Iron Hide takes half of a hit on an ally, Bulwark shelters them, and Last Wall takes the killing blow.
- "Trained and studied": every blow she lands studies its target. She learns you by fighting you.
- "I'll carve a path ahead" is what Trailblaze says, and what Blazed Path does.
- *Reading:* nobody gives her a rank, so her reward in play is only that everybody else is still standing. Setting herself on fire to be faster is a very exact picture of a person who has decided her own cost does not count.

## 7. Alithia Undersen (she/her)

**Looks:** long dark hair with a red flower or ribbon in it, a white dress with blue accents, dark boots, and a staff topped with a red orb. Her confessor mentions fish scales on her neck, which the game has not explained.

**In the game's words:**

> A prideful and arrogant cleric with several capabilities of protecting her allies.
>
> One of the most talented Idol Users of the Holy Order, she always kept the Divine close to her heart as It has guided her many times.
>
> And yet the way people see her are very mixed, for many feel uncomfortable with how much she questions even the smallest thing instead of showing silent obedience. Throughout her studies and career she has infuriated a lot of clerics and yet has also helped the souls of many followers. Due to her debates, the clergy find themselves in an unspoken split between those who consider her blasphemous and those whose faith only became stronger with her masterful questioning.

### Personality

- **Sharp-tongued.** On Cyrus: "He really only understands caveman language." On Enfina: "how do you manage to hang around Cyrus so much and still not pick up how to talk more?" Her staff attack is called Sass Club: "What's worse than getting clobberred by a staff is also hearing a painful remark."
- **Principled, and easily scandalised.** "Wha- Are you both for real right now?! This isn't the reason we are here!" "Aren't you a city guard?!"
- **Protective the moment it matters.** When Prometheus goes quiet over the barrel: "Well, if Prometheus says it's of no importance, then it's of no importance." And to prisoners: "You were only paid. Go."
- **Careful and alert.** "... Hold on. I can hear creatures hiding ahead. If we are to proceed, we should proceed with caution."
- **Holds herself to an impossible standard.** Her confessor: "You've always been really meticulous and never let even the smallest of sin escape from you. Times you lied as a child. Times your anger overtook you and you broke something. Times when you couldn't apologise to someone because of your pride."
- **Cannot stop questioning, even when it destroys her.** "What does it mean for one to be divine? Such a stupid question. And yet here is one who grew up with clergy unable to answer it herself."
- **Angry at what she was told.** "We are misguided, mislead, deceived even and yet in a very sickening way it never was a lie!!! They never explicitly told us what the truth is!"
- **Her flaw, as her confessor names it:** "You try to comprehend and see the entire future. And not even of just yourself. But of everyone."
- **Her power, as he names it:** "you hold the greatest power I've ever seen. You love the entirety of mankind."
- **Proud to the end.** "Holding the truth, only I can uphold the final act to my standards."
- **Sees people clearly.** Her confessor: "You've always looked at others. You were always able to perceive all that they did. That they said. That they felt." She argues with Enfina's comfort and thanks her anyway: "It is not enough to just not be in the wrong. Thank you though." And to the memory of Cyrus: "You're a hero no matter what they say."

*Reading:* her arrogance is real, and it is also the shape her love takes: she thinks she personally has to get it right for everybody. "Prideful" is not a mask over something softer. It is the same thing as her caring, seen from the other side.

### How she talks

Quick, cutting and articulate. She speaks in full sentences and can argue. Sarcasm comes first ("You really can read me like a book, can't you?"), and when that fails, she breaks into something raw and loud. She calls her confessor "mister Asker". When a future she foresaw is changed, she says one of: "I saw it coming." / "Not this time." / "The future bends." / "That is not how it ends."

### The late scene

This is what exists of her story near the end, and it is not a full plot.

- After "the reveal", she has seen something ("I saw the Divine") that she cannot tell anybody, being "forced onto an implied oath of silence". She believes her selfishness may endanger the whole Dome.
- She has tried to end her life, and Aleksander Asker, her personal confessor, stopped her. He loves her "as my daughter". He talks with her through a door: "To live is to suffer... It is because of love that mankind has continued to live. To suffer. And it is the very same thing that makes it all worth it."
- The narration: "She doesn't want to just let go... She wants to live."
- He turns her question back on her: "What does it mean for one to be Divine? Is seeing alone truly believing?"
- Walking the church, she remembers each companion and even the Clown, whom she despises and who makes her realise what she must do. Until then "a cloud" keeps her from leaving.
- The finale: "We've... lost too many. Our very goals have been shattered. This is the end. The messengers have already found the truth. And I'll be the one to finish it after all has been done." In a village square, in front of everybody, she lets a die fall: "The only object that can bring an ending to all of this. The die is cast."

### What has not been said

- What she saw, what "the reveal" is, and what the truth is.
- What the die does. The designer says Moira (Fate) is "actually really important to the story and might end up more important than yalda[baoth] itself".
- Why she has fish scales on her neck, and how she hears creatures the others cannot.
- Who is lost in "We've lost too many".

### In play

**The designer:** "Alithia is the healer with enchantment, illusion and divination spells, allowing her to control the battle field and protect her allies." And on her Idol spells, which ignore part of a target's defence: "the others still have the highest damage outputs but Alithia is specifically better against tanky enemies."

**The game:** "Even though she's really important for the party to not be worn out from the duration of combat, she is durable enough to survive hits herself. Prioritise keeping her in range of everyone!"

Her list of spells is long and varied, so a turn is a question of what the party needs most: mending, control, foresight or a blow. She knows what the enemy will do, she decides what they believe, and she brings people back. She is strongest in Bright places, with Idol light that finds the gaps in armour.

### Abilities

**Costing no gate**

- **Intuition** (1; secondary): reads what an enemy means to do next turn: where they will stand, what they will use, and where it will land, all marked on the map. They are held to it. If the party steps out of the marked tiles, the blow finds nothing, the marks shatter, and "Future changed!" rises off the enemy.

### Where the story and the play meet

- **Her flaw is her mechanic.** Asker says her flaw is trying "to comprehend and see the entire future". Her signature move reads exactly what an enemy will do. When the party changes it, the log says: "The party managed to change the future that Alithia foresaw and the [enemy] is forced to adapt."
- **Coming back costs something.** Resuscitate leaves its mark, and it is Alithia who asks that of her friends.
- **"She does not bend":** Unbowed grows through the whole game, and the last rank comes at level 56, just past ΛΗΘΗ.
- *Reading:* a character named Truth whose path can be illusions, and who works by making enemies believe things, carries a tension the story could use. She deceives her enemies with great skill, and says of herself and her order: "misguided, mislead, deceived even and yet in a very sickening way it never was a lie".
- *Reading:* every blow of hers strips something away (defence, hiding, darkness). Her deepest attack is called Revelation, and it burns through three quarters of what protects you.

## 8. How they are with each other

**Cyrus and Enfina.** Thief and guard. It opens as a chase: "You mongrel! Return the relic you stole!" They know each other already, and within minutes they fight side by side. In the stealth stage where he breaks her out: "You came back for me." / "Stay close, and stay behind me. Where I hide, you hide." ... "We're out. I owe you one." / "You owe me several. Let's go." By late in the story, Alithia notes she hangs around him "so much".

**Cyrus and Prometheus.** A comic double act, the lecture and the heckler ("Human language please!" / "... Is good book."). They scheme over selling the machinery together. "Efficient as ever," says Prometheus when Cyrus turns up supplies.

**Cyrus and Alithia.** She needles him and he needles back. He later tells her: "You are so very petty, do you know that?" Yet his is the memory that makes her say "You're a hero no matter what they say. Don't let them..."

**Prometheus and Alithia.** She covers for him when he won't talk, and calls him a "damn alcoholic" to his face. He offers her a drink when he has no words, and she sees something in him she respects: "you found beauty in remembering."

**Enfina and Alithia.** Opposites in words: the one who says little and the one who cannot stop. Alithia is scandalised by Enfina's practicality, then grateful for her listening, while still disagreeing: "It isn't as simple as that... It is not enough to just not be in the wrong."

**Enfina and Prometheus.** Almost nothing has been written between them yet. In play she shields him, the toughest in front of the frailest.

**All four, deciding together.** After a fight the player decides what happens to the prisoners, and the lines fall this way: Enfina asks ("Well?"), letting them go is Alithia's to say ("Go home. Find honest work."), handing them over is Prometheus's ("The watch can have them."), and Cyrus is the one who notices who got away.

**In play, they lean on each other:**

- Enfina holds the front, and Prometheus burns what she is holding.
- Alithia keeps everybody up, reads the enemy's next move, and points out the hidden.
- Cyrus runs for the casters, spreads what lingers, and uses whatever the party is carrying.
- Prometheus moves everybody else about the board with Hermes Step, Hover and Growth.
- Light divides them: Alithia is at her best in Bright, Cyrus shoots out of the Dark into it, and Prometheus can make either.

## 9. Lines still to be written: speech bubbles

The designer wanted, in their words, "dialogue that only shows up as a bubble above a character that goes away by itself", with lines "for every character depending on what happens in combat". It is built, and the designer has been writing the lines into it (below). It replaces an earlier idea of tracking what Enfina does for others in a quiet tally.

They have to be short, since they fade by themselves mid-fight, and they have to sound like the person saying them. Each can have several lines for a moment, which take turns. These are the moments the game can already use:

- A fight starts, and the first blow lands.
- They fell somebody, or an ally falls.
- They are badly hurt, or healed by somebody in particular.
- They are burned, frozen, stunned or frightened.
- They take a blow for somebody, or a reaction goes off.
- An illusion is seen through, or a foreseen future changes.
- A deep spell, or their last casting.
- A boss changes phase, and the fight is won.
- Using one move in particular, or one condition in particular landing: Enfina studying somebody, and Enfina setting herself alight.
- **Studying a monster, per monster** (new): each monster has one line for Enfina and one for Prometheus, said the first time in a fight they study one of its kind. None written yet.
- **Written by the designer, 2026-11-13, for all four:** a big hit taken and given (half or more of somebody's health in one blow), being brought back, having something harmful lifted, an enemy breaking and running (one of them speaks), out of castings (Alithia: "I can no longer protect you. Do not squander the chance I gave you!"; Prometheus: "My system is out of power, I leave the rest to you."), praise for each other's kills (a line for each pair, e.g. Cyrus to Prometheus "Burn them to the ground, bud!", Alithia to Cyrus "Finally making yourself useful?", Enfina to Alithia "They don't stand a chance against your holy relics."), and Enfina's Step In ("I'm right here.", "You can't escape me.", "Can you really afford to be distracted?"). Their voices: Cyrus cocky and joking ("I've got 9 more where that came from!"), Prometheus terse and steady ("I'm... alright."), Alithia proud and sharp ("You bore me."), Enfina hard and quiet ("Is that all...?").

The designer has written some already: Enfina's ("So that's how you fight.", "Watch the feet.", "Seen that before." studying; "You are not getting away.", "It's only fire.", "I'll carve the path ahead." with Trailblaze; "Go. I've got it." sharing the fire; "I'm right here.", "You can't escape me." and "Can you really afford to be distracted?" stepping in to take a blow for somebody; an earlier "Mine." was taken out, the designer's call of 2026-11-13) and Alithia's when she changes the future ("Didn't expect to let you do that, did you now?", "Not this time.", "Our actions shape our future!", "So very obvious of you."). More places lines could go are in docs/proposals_2026-11-07.md, section 19.

## 10. Open for discussion

- **Enfina's voice in play.** She has the least written so far and is the one being deepened, so her lines and her story matter most right now.
- **Her lack of recognition.** Does anybody ever finally say something to her, and who?
- **Cyrus and being hidden.** Hitting from hiding gives no bonus at present, only that nobody can react, and if the blow fells them and nobody else sees, he stays hidden. His passive Night Hunter (from 24): felling somebody from hiding on his own turn, he slips out of sight again, even while they watch, once a turn.
- **The world's weather is a rule now.** Rain soaks (ice and lightning hurt more), wind carries shots along it and shortens them across it, deep snow is slow going, lightning strikes in a thunderstorm, fog and sandstorms hide whoever knows how. Guards hear steps further in a light rain and less in a storm, and follow fresh prints in mud and snow.
- **Necromancy for Prometheus.** Whether he uses it, how he came to know it, and what Alithia does about it.
- **What the four know about each other,** and when each learns about the others' pasts.
