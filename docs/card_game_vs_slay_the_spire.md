# What Slay the Spire has that the card game does not (2026-11-27)

The designer, 2026-11-27: "what else does slay the spire have that we don't have? For example there are characters?". Slay the
Spire (2019) unless it says Slay the Spire 2 (in Early Access, changing with its patches). Where one of the world twists of
`docs/proposals_2026-11-26_card_game.md` would be our own version of it, the twist is named. Nothing here is built.

The card game now: one deck (10 starting cards, 28 cards to win), one act of 12 floors, 19 enemies in 20 fights, one boss, 12
relics, 4 events, Status cards in a fight, the enemies' rules and traits, a Challenge of 0 to 3, the juice.

## Who you play

- **Characters.** Slay the Spire has four (the Ironclad, the Silent, the Defect, the Watcher); Slay the Spire 2 has five (adding
  the Regent and the Necrobinder) and a newer one in beta. Each has its own health, starting deck, starting relic, about 75 cards
  of its own and a mechanic of its own: Strength, Exhaust and self-harm; Poison, Shivs and discarding; orbs and Focus; stances
  and Mantra. A shared pool of colourless cards sits beside them. Ours: one deck and no character to choose. (Twist 5, four
  suits, is ours: the party's four as the characters.)

## In a fight

- **Potions:** three slots, found after fights and bought in shops, used on your turn (mend, Block, damage, energy, draw, a buff,
  a debuff). In Slay the Spire 2 co-op you can throw one to a teammate. Ours: none.
- **Card keywords:** Innate (starts in your hand), Retain (stays in your hand), Ethereal (we have it only on Status cards), X-cost
  (spends all your energy), cards that make other cards (Shivs), discarding and cards that pay off on it, Scry (look at the top
  of your draw pile and throw away what you do not want), cards that exhaust others, cards that change costs.
- **Curses:** bad cards that stay in your deck for the run, from events and chests, until removed. Ours: Status cards last a
  fight only.
- **More statuses:** Artifact (stops the next debuff), Intangible (every hit does 1), Plated Armor, Regen, Buffer, Barricade (Block
  kept). (Strength, Dexterity, Vulnerable, Weak, Frail, Poison, Metallicize, Thorns and Ritual are ours as Might, Footing,
  Exposed, Weakened, Brittle, Poison, Ward, Spines and Frenzy.)

## The run

- **Three acts** (and the Heart behind a fourth): each with its own enemies, elites, events and three possible bosses, healing
  between them. Ours: one act, one boss. (Twist 1, climbing to the Dome's ceiling, would be our acts.)
- **Boss relics:** after a boss, one of three strong relics, many with a drawback (an extra energy a turn, at a cost).
- **A start of a run:** Neow's blessing (a bonus, sometimes for a price); in Slay the Spire 2, an Ancient's relics at the start of
  every act. Ours: a set first relic. (Twist 15, the Blessings, would be ours.)
- **About 180 relics** with many moments (on an attack, on every so many cards, on shuffling, on exhausting, on losing health,
  out of a fight). Ours: 12, with five moments.
- **About 50 events** with more kinds of outcome: transforming a card into a random other, duplicating one, a curse for a prize,
  a fight inside an event. Ours: 4, and no transform.
- **Shops** sell potions and colourless cards too, with one card at half price. **Rests** gain choices from relics (lifting,
  smoking a card away, digging up a relic). **Question-mark rooms** can turn out a fight, a shop or a chest. **Chests** come in
  three sizes. **Rare cards** grow likelier the longer none is offered.

## Around the run

- **Saving a run:** leave at any time and carry on later. Ours: leaving loses the run.
- **Ascension:** 20 levels, each adding one hardship. Ours: Challenge 0 to 3.
- **Unlocks:** characters unlocked by playing, cards and relics by score; a Compendium of everything seen, run history,
  statistics, a score at a run's end, achievements.
- **Other ways to play:** the Daily Climb (one seeded run a day with modifiers, and a leaderboard), Custom runs (choose the
  modifiers), a seed typed in.
- **Co-op** (Slay the Spire 2): see `docs/proposals_2026-11-27_card_coop.md`.
